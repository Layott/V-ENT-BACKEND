## 1. The decision, and why it is not a fork

Repainting every organiser's graphics in one client's brand is not the answer,
so **a broadcast now carries a LOOK**. `BroadcastSession.theme`, `vent` by
default, `rivalry` for this pack. The console has a Design control at the top of
the house style block.

A look, not a fork:

- the same components read the same feed, and only the drawing changes
- a correction to what a card SAYS reaches both looks and they cannot drift
- anything not drawn in a look falls through to the house drawing, so a graphic
  is never a black browser source at the moment somebody cues it

**The look is in the feed version.** An element page skips its redraw when the
version has not moved, so without that a broadcast switched mid show would keep
drawing the old look until something else happened to change. That has already
happened twice here, to squad depth and again to the rivalry block.

## 4. The harness, which is the part worth keeping

`V-ENT-FRONTEND/scripts/overlay-parity.mjs` shoots every ported graphic at
1920x1080 beside the original it came from, into
`V-ENT/tasks/audit/overlay-parity/`.

    WALK_TOKEN=<studio session token> node scripts/overlay-parity.mjs
    WALK_TOKEN=... node scripts/overlay-parity.mjs standings head_to_head

Both sides at native size, no scaling, fonts awaited before the shot. A
broadcast graphic looked at in a narrower window is a graphic whose design is
cropped, and every measurement then looks right while being wrong on air.

**It found the fault no amount of reading would have.** Four of the six ports
painted a full bleed background that the approved graphic does not have. The
python generators pass `ground=` and `opaque=True` to `render()`, and the
SHIPPED FILES contain no `<img class="bg">` and no `opaque` class at all: it did
not survive into the approved build. On air that background would have covered
the entire video feed. Every agent had read the generator carefully and every
one of them got it wrong the same way.

Check it on any element of the pack like this:

    python -c "import re,pathlib;t=pathlib.Path('A3 standings.html').read_text(errors='ignore');
    print(re.findall(r'<div class=\"frame[^\"]*\"',t), len(re.findall(r'class=\"bg\"',t)))"
